------------------------------------------------------------
USER
  ↓
authenticate()
  ↓
JWT verified
  ↓
authorize("ADMIN")
  ↓
No ADMIN role
  ↓
403 Forbidden
------------------------------------------------------------
ADMIN
  ↓
authenticate()
  ↓
JWT verified
  ↓
authorize("ADMIN")
  ↓
ADMIN role found
  ↓
adminOnly()
  ↓
200 OK

Notice that the role is coming from the JWT payload:
req.user = {
  userId: payload.userId,
  email: payload.email,
  roles: payload.roles,
};

and authorize() checks:
const hasRequiredRole =
  req.user.roles.some((role) =>
    allowedRoles.includes(role),
  );

So we have successfully separated:
Authentication
Who is the user?

from
Authorization
What is the user allowed to do?
